# Superstore Sales Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing Superstore sales data using Microsoft Power BI. The objective is to understand sales performance, profitability, customer segments, product categories, and regional sales distribution through an interactive dashboard.

## Objectives
- Analyze overall sales and profit performance.
- Identify the highest-performing customer segments.
- Compare sales across product categories.
- Understand regional sales distribution.
- Identify opportunities to improve business profitability.

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Power BI
- Superstore Sales Dataset

## Dashboard Features
- Total Sales, Total Profit, Quantity, and Profit Margin KPIs.
- Sales analysis by customer segment.
- Profit analysis by product category and subcategory.
- Regional quantity analysis.
- Discount analysis by customer segment.
- Country and category performance table.
- Business Insights page with key findings and recommendations.

## Key Business Insights
- Consumer customers contribute approximately 50.32% of total sales.
- Technology is the highest-selling product category.
- West is the leading region in quantity sold.
- The overall profit margin is approximately 12.55%.
- Discount optimization and improved product profitability are important business opportunities.

## Conclusion
The dashboard provides an overview of Superstore business performance and helps identify opportunities for improving sales, profitability, and business decision-making.
